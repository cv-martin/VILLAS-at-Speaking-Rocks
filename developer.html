Engineering & Development

A Small Enclave, Built With Engineering Discipline

Villas at Speaking Rock is engineered by Rao's Consulting Engineers (TXPE Firm #17655) and Rickman Highland Surveying, with the site plan, utility design, and grading engineered specifically for this 10.76-acre tract under Travis County Final Plat Reference FP-23-0129.

Our Approach

Engineered for This Land, Specifically

Speaking Rock was not planned from a template. Every element of the site — grading, drainage, street layout, and lot lines — was engineered around the terrain and tree canopy already on this tract.

1

Site-Specific Engineering

Grading, drainage, and utility plans are designed around this tract's existing topography and tree survey — not a repeated lot pattern.

2

Careful Entitlement Process

Speaking Rock has moved through Concept Plan, Preliminary Plat, PUD, and Final Plat review (FP-23-0129) with Travis County.

3

Small by Design

A limited estate enclave. The scale was chosen deliberately to preserve privacy and mature tree canopy on the tract.

4

Hill Country Sensibility

Materials, Italian street naming, and private cul-de-sacs are drawn from the timeless language established at Travisso.
